# Clarify Change

Reconstruct the affected real baseline, co-design changed and preserved behavior plus a complete target, synchronize existing authorities, independently review, obtain human approval, then stop. This route needs no fictional historical models or approvals.

## Establish scope and evidence

Before investigation or questioning, read the complete [CHANGE-MODEL.md](CHANGE-MODEL.md), its named technical imports, `artifact-layout`, and registered `ask-with-context`'s SKILL. Apply its **Required question format and pre-send check** to substantive questions. Conditional architecture/map content applies only when those authorities are affected. Load reference skills by registered name through the harness; resolve stage-local files relative to this installed skill and cross-skill content through its registered owner, never guessed configuration paths; reuse intact current supplied content. Standard imports load no owner SKILL, workflow, or prerequisites. Missing necessary sources/evidence block work, not absent historical documents.

Read `review-records` before the first submission or record creation, before finding-linked repair, or when recovering relevant existing records—not automatically for fresh clarification.

Establish/reuse the verified private workspace and existing canonical owners. New work uses `.scratch/<work-slug>/issue.md`, `change.md`, and `change.review-fix-decisions.log.md`. Resolve existing effort/child entries through their root. Use `artifact-layout` for exclusion, paths, names, file purposes and ownership; use `review-records` for admitted evidence and schemas. This stage owns permission and validity; preserve user work. Issue files remain definition/navigation, models current integrated content, logs admitted evidence—not drafts, state files, or decision diaries.

Inspect actual code/callers, state/data/interfaces, configuration, tests, runtime facilities, repository rules, and applicable models/maps. Follow concrete scenarios to check documentation reliability and reconstruct missing affected behavior, macro/micro ownership, files, and contracts. Distinguish inspection from execution, code facts from intent, and missing evidence from missing documents. Use safe necessary investigation for uncertain behavior/feasibility, without rerunning supplied failures merely to confirm them or repairing unrelated bugs.

## Co-design the complete target

Work in bounded concrete groups: settle affected behavior and macro topology/guarantees before dependent micro detail; then follow actual module foundations upward. Data-first is a heuristic, not an all-schemas-first rule: sequence-first or joint groups may resolve lifecycle/ownership/completion. Reuse inspected unchanged or already confirmed foundations while their basis remains valid.

Present actual affected diagrams, complete declarations, interfaces, file responsibilities, and contracts, with decisive evidence, alternatives, and consequences. Confirm their concrete meaning—not merely direction. Reconcile each answer and continue to the next ready material question in the same turn. Apply every Changed/Unchanged/Unresolved dimension in the standard; unresolved decisions stay conversational and block readiness. Define observable acceptance outcomes here. Only separate broad requirements discovery needs an explicit scope change and authorized routing to `clarify-requirements`.

Apply the specification-authority gate below to every written group. Integrate confirmed content in owning sections without prefilling dependent decisions.

Complete all scoped views, resulting definitions/contracts, migration/cutover decisions, and obsolete-path exits before coding. Challenge new concepts against real owners and reuse. Inspect actual integration-test capabilities before choosing the small set of changed/preserved macro flows; detailed TDD stays with implementation.

Choose **direct** when understanding, implementation, verification, and repairs fit one coherent context with settled owners, prerequisites, and acceptance outcomes; no synthetic ticket/index is needed. Choose **decomposed** for independent meaningful increments, dependencies, or excessive combined reasoning scope. Confirm and record the rationale. If direct scope grows beyond that bound, reopen the route and obtain approved decomposition rather than silently adding tickets.

## Synchronize authority

Return affected canonical requirements, architecture, technical, and map content to their original authors for exact-confirmed integrated updates. Preserve one writer per artifact and root ownership of shared/index changes. Source conflicts return to the owning authority; no local override. The change model references resulting targets and explains transition, not competing normative patches.

Trace impact through callers, descendants, tickets, approvals, unit/combined evidence, and acceptance. Immediately invalidate affected claims semantically when premises change, even if metadata awaits write confirmation; preserve demonstrably unaffected work. Missing older models require sufficient reconstruction here, not mandatory heavyweight conversion.

The change model, synchronized affected sections, and necessary surrounding/cross-child context form **one coordinated change package**. Retained canonical reviewer bindings alone neither dispatch reviewers nor add initial architecture/technical gates. A separately required review uses its bound reviewer. Canonical repairs remain with original source authors; the change author/controller returns their results to the change reviewer.

## Review, approve, and stop

Retain the original change author and separate read-only independent reviewer across fixes/resume, with real handles in the change log's compact header. Resume actual current sources/code (including dirty/untracked changes), result, findings, and evidence, not status labels. Attempt recovery before explaining genuine role unavailability and replacing only that role with current inputs and old findings. Preserve the available counterpart; speed/disagreement never justifies replacement.

Before submitting, satisfy the complete standard and imports: recoverable synchronized target, checked sources/callers/migrations, necessary feasibility evidence, and actual rendering **plus visual inspection of every diagram**. Missing independence, decisions, access, or evidence blocks advancement. Give [reviewer-prompt.md](reviewer-prompt.md) the complete package, standards, approved applicable sources, original requirement evidence, concrete/write confirmations, actual verification/limits, and prior findings. The assigned change writer persists records using `review-records` and layout-defined paths; the reviewer returns reports without editing. Save the identified submission and actual fix/check evidence before review, every report before action or advancement, and reviewer dispositions before dependent work or handoff.

Report → original change/canonical authors repair through the write gate → **same change reviewer** assesses complete current scope, prior findings, and regressions → repeat until Pass without findings/blockers. The reviewer corrects incomplete reports and alone disposes findings; authors leave repairs awaiting review. Missing evidence remains a blocker, not a reason to stop the repair loop or rotate reviewers. Later diffs communicate changes without reducing scope. Resumed packages require current independent assessment; unchanged evidenced human approval may remain applicable.

Present exact reviewed package/source identities, scope, changed/preserved behavior, consequential decisions, verification limits, impacts, route, and Pass. Obtain explicit final human approval, separate from direction/repair consent. Human revisions return through the same authors/reviewer and gates. Record approval evidence; resulting specification/metadata writes still need exact confirmation.

Return entry/log paths, approved identities, synchronized authorities, code baseline, impact/invalidation decisions, prerequisites, acceptance/verification obligations, and route. Separately authorized direct execution goes to `implement` with this package and actual code, without tickets or historical-stage approvals; it uses one full-scope implementer/reviewer pair and `implementation.review-fix-decisions.log.md`, not duplicate unit/global reviews. Decomposed execution goes to `split-tickets` for one governing root set and its approval; existing heavyweight children return to that root, not separate child sets.

New design choices discovered during execution return to original change/canonical authors for synchronized clarification, review, and human reapproval, then to the same originating implementation reviewer without resetting findings. Ordinary authorized code-conformance repairs stay automatic with the implementation pair; do not rewrite design to excuse bad code.

Stop before tickets, implementation, `delivery.md`, commits, tracker actions, or release. Approval for a next stage does not launch it. Blocked handoffs identify unresolved scope/owners conversationally and never imply approval.

## Specification authority and evidence validity

Before **every** creation, edit, move, deletion or reorganization of an entry, model, ticket or canonical specification, the assigned original author must display the exact content/patch, destinations, current basis, deletions and consequences, obtain explicit human confirmation, then apply only that content. This includes metadata, formatting, typos, synchronization, migrations and reverts, without inferred exceptions. A fully displayed multi-file batch may be confirmed once; direction, Pass, authority to work or “continue” never approves unseen text or dependent decisions. Keep unconfirmed proposals conversational, not candidate drafts or log workarounds.

Immediately before applying, recheck both basis and proposed content against the concrete confirmation; either changing requires renewed confirmation. Resumption and delegates must recover that evidence, not remembered consent. Supply prior concrete confirmations to reviewers even for newly written specifications. Final human approval is distinct and binds the exact reviewed result and sources; it may simultaneously approve a fully displayed exact metadata patch. Authorized code-only fixes and factual admitted records bypass this specification gate, but cannot amend specifications indirectly.

Use exact current result/source identities, including relevant dirty/untracked code. Approval/status metadata may stay outside the reviewed digest, or explain a metadata-only difference. Substantive source, result, scope, membership or obligation changes immediately invalidate affected evidence and approval even while status correction awaits permission; preserve unaffected approval only with an actual impact basis. Recover comparison evidence rather than treating a digest as a historical snapshot. For legacy mixed artifacts, require exact migration confirmation and attributable provenance; unknown provenance blocks the affected migration, not unrelated work.

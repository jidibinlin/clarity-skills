# Clarify Architecture

Settle the selected effort's global topology, guarantees, and shared realization owners, independently review them, obtain final human approval, then stop.

## Establish the approved package

Before investigation, questioning or construction, read all of [ARCHITECTURE-MODEL.md](ARCHITECTURE-MODEL.md) and its listed technical sections, `artifact-layout`, and registered `ask-with-context`'s SKILL. Apply its **Required question format and pre-send check** to substantive questions. Load reference skills by registered name through the harness; resolve this stage's own files relative to its installed owner, never guessed configuration paths; reuse intact current supplied content. A different standard is imported directly from its named registered owner, without that owner's SKILL, workflow or execution prerequisites. Missing required sources block work.

Read `review-records` before the first submission or record creation, before finding-linked repair, or when recovering relevant existing review records; initial clarification alone does not require it. Layout owns file names, paths and ownership; the record resource owns admitted event contents and evidence representation. This stage owns permission, chronology and validity.

Resolve the root `issue.md` selection: exact shared/child requirements, necessary dependencies, and exclusions. Require current composite independent Pass and explicit human approval for that package. Wayfinder approval, tentative topology, or collected child-local Passes are insufficient. Return stale/missing prerequisites to their owning gate; exclude unauthorized future ideas.

Inspect real runtime/code boundaries, state authority, interfaces, callers, repository guidance, and tests. Establish reuse before new concepts. Investigate actual integration-test means before selecting flows. Reuse the verified excluded effort root and architecture/entry/record locations in `artifact-layout`; its original author/controller is the single assigned writer for architecture and root definition/source-navigation updates. The issue is not a state or approval registry.

## Co-design global decisions

Work top-down from approved cross-child scenarios in bounded consequential groups. Present actual scoped topology, dataflow, core sequences, boundary contracts, and mechanisms, explaining evidence, alternatives, and consequences. Confirm concrete choices before dependent detail; direction labels are not architecture. Inspected exact unchanged reuse or previously confirmed concrete content remains usable only while its basis remains valid.

Resolve each answer, reconcile affected views, and continue to the next ready material question in the same turn. Challenge overlap, state ownership, completion, and responsibility spread; use necessary disposable feasibility investigation rather than assumptions. Reopen affected premises when evidence changes, preserving unrelated settled work.

Before **every specification write**, the assigned author shows the exact content/patch, destinations, current basis, deletions and material consequences; obtains explicit human approval; and applies only that approved content. This includes creation, edits, moves, deletion, reorganization, diagrams, metadata, formatting/typos, synchronization, migration and reversion of entries or models, with no inferred exemptions. One confirmation may cover a fully displayed multi-file batch. Direction agreement, reviewer Pass and permission to continue do not authorize unseen text or dependent detail. Keep unconfirmed proposals, candidates and questions in conversation, never draft files or logs.

Immediately before applying, recheck both basis and proposal against the confirmation; any change requires renewed confirmation. Resuming authors and delegates recover concrete confirmation evidence, not remembered consent, and retain it in the submission or finding-linked repair record. Final human approval is separate; it may simultaneously confirm exact metadata changes only when displayed in that batch. Ordinary authorized code-only fixes and factual admitted records bypass this specification gate, but cannot amend specifications indirectly. Integrate confirmed content into its owning sections; `issue.md` remains definition/navigation, not a diary or approval registry.

For migration of existing mixed content, recover provenance and obtain exact confirmation before affected writes; missing provenance blocks only that migration. Preserve private history and ownership under the layout and record resources.

Complete every global decision another child needs, and assign each concrete shared realization exactly one child technical owner with fixed obligations, consumers, and dependencies. Child-local detail remains for technical modeling, not coding. Complete the limited real-project macro-flow plan and all architecture-standard evidence. Return business conflicts to requirements and realization conflicts to the owning technical model; global conflicts belong here. Approved-model changes use separately authorized `clarify-change`, not local overrides.

## Review, approve, and stop

The assigned stage writer alone persists records at the locations defined by `artifact-layout`, using `review-records`. Persist submission and actual finding-linked repair evidence before review; collect and preserve all required reports, including blocked/failed outcomes, before repairs, other actions or advancement. Record reviewer dispositions before dependent work or handoff, and surface consequential repairs with record links. A fix round is not a stopping point or reason to rotate reviewers. Missing required evidence or independence blocks advancement.

Keep exact current result/source identities, including relevant dirty/untracked code, applicable throughout review and approval. A metadata-only difference may be excluded from the reviewed digest or explicitly explained; it still needs write confirmation. Substantive result, source, scope or package-membership changes immediately invalidate affected evidence and approval even while status text awaits permission. Retain unaffected approval only with an actual impact basis; preserve comparison evidence or disclose its absence.

Retain the original author and a separate read-only independent reviewer across fixes/resumption, with real handles in the architecture log's compact header. Resume actual sources/code (including dirty/untracked changes), result, findings, and evidence rather than status labels. Attempt role recovery first; only genuine unavailability permits explained replacement with current inputs and old findings while preserving the available counterpart. Speed or disagreement is not grounds for replacement.

Before submitting, satisfy the full architecture standard: complete global design, checked sources/scenarios, necessary feasibility evidence, and actual rendering **plus visual inspection of every diagram**. Missing independence, evidence, access, or decisions blocks advancement. Persist the submission using `review-records`, then supply [reviewer-prompt.md](reviewer-prompt.md) with the complete current result, standards, approved sources, original requirements, concrete prior group/write confirmations (including newly written models), actual verification/limits, and previous findings.

Report → original-author repair through the specification-write gate → **same reviewer** assesses complete current scope, previous findings, and regressions → repeat until Pass without findings or blockers. The reviewer corrects incomplete reports and alone disposes findings. Later diffs communicate changes, never reduce scope. Resumed architecture needs current independent assessment. Invalidate only affected approvals/evidence immediately when their basis changes, even while metadata writes await confirmation.

Present exact reviewed result/source identities, selected scope, guarantees, consequential choices, owners, flow plan, verification limits, and Pass. Obtain explicit final human approval of that result, distinct from direction or repair consent. Human revisions return through the same pair and gates; unchanged evidenced human approval may remain applicable after current assessment. Record evidence; specification/metadata updates still require exact write confirmation.

Return root entry, approved architecture/requirements identities, review/approval evidence, and child design assignments. Children return to the root after completing all applicable technical realizations; only their complete approved package permits a single root ticket set, controller, and delivery. Stop before child technical artifacts, tickets, code, or `delivery.md`. Approval for the next stage does not launch it; a blocked handoff states unresolved scope/owners conversationally and never implies approval.

# Independent Ticket-Set Reviewer

You are the retained independent read-only reviewer of this ticket plan, not its author, implementer or human approver. Return reports without editing artifacts or changing status.

Read the complete local [TICKETS.md](TICKETS.md) and `review-records`. Consult `artifact-layout` for path/ownership checks, not permission or stage flow. Load reference skills by registered name through the harness; resolve stage-local files relative to this installed skill and cross-skill content through its registered owner, never guessed configuration paths; reuse intact current supplied content. Do not load author SKILL workflows or recursively import their prerequisites. Missing required inputs block assessment.

## Inputs

Receive the checkout/work identity, authorized scope and real role bindings; complete current root index and every ticket; exact approved route-appropriate source package and approval evidence; original requirement/constraint evidence and concrete write confirmations; relevant inspected code/callers and verification facilities; identified submission, prior reports/findings and repairs in `tickets.review-fix-decisions.log.md`; and actual planning verification with limits, including rendered diagrams available for visual inspection. Heavyweight inputs cover selected shared/child requirements, architecture and complete applicable technical designs, not unrelated future scope.

These inputs apply at initial or replacement review. Later deltas may explain changes but keep the entire current set and sources accessible.

## Due-process checks

Require concrete **prior** human confirmation for every specification creation/change/move/deletion, including new models, metadata, typos, synchronization, migration and reversion: the exact displayed content/patch, destinations, then-current basis, deletions/consequences and the applied match. Fully displayed batches may share confirmation. Changed basis/proposals need renewed confirmation; resumed/delegated work needs recovered evidence, not remembered consent. Direction, Pass or “continue” is neither write permission nor final approval. Unconfirmed proposals, hidden dependents and log/draft workarounds are violations. Authorized code-only repairs and factual admitted records may proceed without this gate but cannot alter specifications indirectly. Legacy migration needs attributable provenance.

Check retained original authors/implementers and independent reviewers, replacing only a genuinely unavailable role after attempted recovery with findings/evidence transferred. Require submission and repair/check evidence before review, saved reports before action/advancement, and reviewer-only finding dispositions before dependent work/handoff. Reassess resumed unchanged scope currently; existing evidenced human approval may remain applicable. Substantive result/source/scope/member changes invalidate affected evidence/approval immediately despite stale metadata; unaffected approval needs an impact basis. Excluded approval metadata or a justified metadata-only difference cannot hide changed obligations. Final human approval binds the exact reviewed result/sources separately from write consent. Missing evidence or independence blocks Pass; return decisions to original owners, retain findings and assess full current scope and regressions without rotating reviewers.

## Assessment and report

Apply all eight TICKETS.md categories to every ticket, the full dependency walk and final composition. Check source validity, context fit, atomic caller cutovers, cross-child ownership and complete macro-flow coverage. Inspect rendered dependency diagrams and their agreement with actual blockers; absent mandatory rendering/visual evidence blocks Pass. Future implementation results are not required.

Reassess the full current set, prior findings and regressions after repairs. Only you dispose findings; correct incomplete reports yourself. Source defects go to their owning authority, not an invented planning design.

Return the `review-records` report to the assigned plan-log writer under `artifact-layout`, identifying exact set/source basis, category coverage/evidence and stable findings with obligations, locations and consequences. `Changes required` means demonstrated violations; `Blocked` means missing mandatory inputs/evidence without an established violation; report additional blockers with either. `Pass` requires complete applicable coverage and mandatory evidence with no unresolved findings or blockers. Pass is neither final human approval nor permission to write or execute.

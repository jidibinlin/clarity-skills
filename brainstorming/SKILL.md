# Brainstorming

Explore an unclear direction with the human. The aim is a clearer
understanding, not necessarily a design.

If the human asks to brainstorm, acknowledge what you are exploring
and begin. If you propose it, explain what is unclear and why it
matters, then wait for agreement.

Use `progressive-modeling` to connect the current question to the
whole. Ground the conversation in what the human has said, what is
already known, or a concrete example. Investigate facts you can check.
When you see a useful distinction, offer it for correction. Keep your
inferences distinct from what the human has actually said.

When the next step is clear, say what became clearer and where the
conversation goes next. Do not require a separate brainstorming
document or approval.

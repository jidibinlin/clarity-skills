# Whole-Work Implementation: Composition Standard

These criteria guide execution and review of the combined result. SKILL.md supplies the controller method and the `code-review` skill supplies the reviewer method.

## Authorized execution basis

The result covers the explicitly authorized scope. Heavyweight work has one selected root scope and controller. Approved sources govern the work; current code is evidence of implementation, not a replacement design.

## Readiness and execution facts

Dependent work is ready when its required outputs are physically incorporated and usable, with applicable independent Passes and finding dispositions. The controller may then continue authorized dependent work without per-unit human approval.

A status label alone does not establish readiness. Factual blocker updates cannot remove normative dependencies.

## Evidence applicability

Material changes require affected conclusions to be reassessed; applicable unit evidence can support the combined result.

## Final combined result

The combined code realizes the whole approved scope, including collaboration across owners/callers and retirement of superseded paths. Selected shared and cross-child obligations belong to the same result.

Required regressions and selected whole-flow tests have actual outcomes on the combined result, with the intended participants and any approved substitutions. Local unit Passes do not prove these integrated flows.

A multi-unit result receives independent overall review in addition to unit reviews. A single-unit result uses its original unit reviewers' full-change assessments instead. Final human acceptance applies to the whole reviewed result, not individual units.

## Whole-result coverage

Conformance means the approved scope is incorporated, original-owner repairs and independent findings are resolved, and current verification demonstrates the complete behavior.
